## Proposed representation rules

The proposed frame is a review sidecar, not a Router feature, accepted role slot, new gold or automatic state decision. It carries literal evidence kinds GOAL, SUBJECT, DEMAND, CONTENT, MEANS, BACKGROUND, RECIPIENT and QUALIFIER, plus exact copies of every original qualifier/antecedent. DEMAND is kept separate from an explicit main operation. Each of these19 writer judgments currently records no unique explicit main-operation span; this is a reasoned proposal, not a mechanically established grammatical fact.

1. A nominal meaning, comparison, design, editing or scheduling goal may identify a current matter without inventing understand/compare/draft/schedule operators. Lack of an imperative does not automatically produce DEFER.
2. Current I need/I want/需要/只需要 identifies demand modality. It is not silently accepted as ACTION. Background needs, want or a past request does not substitute for the current demand occurrence.
3. Requested messages, checklists, frameworks, directions and rules retain their output goal and sustained subject. Format alone does not decide Topic. Recipient, input subject and requested artifact are different evidence layers.
4. Actions contained in a reply/rule/framework or inside an explanation describe its required content. A by/using clause may provide a method. Neither creates a distinct current target or external execution permission without actual evidence.
5. Current correction and explicit exclusions remain. Prior calculation, not-an-agenda, no-real-service/repository/backup, not-sending and classroom/fictional constraints cannot be erased by choosing a trailing goal.
6. These frames do not change any expected state/set, accepted Topic, label boundary, source input, compiled role or consumption ledger. Real semantic acceptance and independence remain separate prerequisites.
